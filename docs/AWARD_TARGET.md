# Award target: CSS Design Awards

## Recommendation

Target CSS Design Awards (CSSDA), submitting as a solo designer with one collaborator if applicable. Its UI Design, UX Design, Innovation, and Website of the Day award paths fit an original, interactive educational site. The site's original narrative, custom diagrams, and code-linked interactions are relevant to the stated judging categories.

## Current published requirements (checked 28 October 2026)

- The official submission page lists a **$50 USD** submission fee and says it includes eight award opportunities, including UI Design, UX Design, Innovation, Website of the Day, Website of the Month, Website of the Year, Special Kudos, and the matching Designer of the Year category.
- The work must be complete and original. The submission requires accurate site information and a high-quality screenshot.
- The current submission flow specifies a **1068 × 646 px JPG**, maximum **150 KB**, and asks for the public website URL, a short description, category, and up to three tags.
- The public award route combines votes and panel scores; the About page describes the judging thresholds and the WOTD panel process.
- The official pages reviewed do not publish a fixed annual deadline; the submission flow appears open. Confirm this again when the site is live and ready to enter.
