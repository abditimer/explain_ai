# Reader test invitation

Copy and adapt this invitation when recruiting participants. The test is most useful for people who have not studied transformer models.

> Could you spend about 10 minutes trying a short website that explains how an AI model continues a sentence? No technical background is needed; I’m especially looking for people who have not studied transformer models. I’ll ask you to explore the site and explain what you think is happening. This is feedback on the explanation, not a test of you. I won’t record audio or collect identifying information. You can stop at any time.

## Before the session

- Schedule five individual sessions, 8–12 minutes each.
- Use the public site: https://abditimer.github.io/explain_ai/
- Have the [moderator guide](READER_TEST_GUIDE.md) and one blank [anonymous session record](READER_TEST_RECORD_TEMPLATE.md) ready.
- Label each record `R01` through `R05`; keep scheduling/contact details separate from notes and discard them after arranging the session.
- Do not explain the concepts, correct answers, or steer participants toward the code during the tasks.
